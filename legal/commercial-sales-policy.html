Commercial Sales Policy

Negotiated commercial model

Commercial source licensing for UPE is negotiated. Public website materials are informational and do not create binding pricing or rights by themselves.

No rights until agreement

No commercial rights are granted until the parties execute a written agreement. Quotes, conversations, and public descriptions are not a substitute for that agreement.

Digital delivery and finality

Because commercial source packages are digital and sensitive, transactions are generally final once delivery has occurred, unless a signed agreement states otherwise.

Compliance

Customers are responsible for their own export, regulatory, and internal compliance obligations relating to their use of licensed materials.
