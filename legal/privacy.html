Privacy Policy

Information collected

Arctic Codex may collect contact information, company details, and inquiry content submitted through forms or direct email. Basic analytics, server logs, or cookie-based performance data may also be collected if enabled.

How the data is used

Inquiry data is used to respond to licensing requests, technical review requests, support questions, or collaboration inquiries. It may also be used to improve the website and documentation experience.

Sharing and disclosure

Inquiry data is not sold as a marketing list. It may be shared only with service providers or advisors reasonably involved in operating the site or evaluating a commercial request, subject to normal confidentiality expectations.

Retention and contact

Data may be retained as long as reasonably needed for business, legal, support, or recordkeeping purposes. Privacy questions may be sent to arcticasters@gmail.com.
