Plain-English commercial summary

Licensing should feel straightforward.

UPE is licensed, not sold by default. The primary posture is a non-exclusive commercial source license for integration into customer products, while ownership of UPE itself remains with BearPack / Arctic Codex unless a separate deal says otherwise.

Exclusivity and acquisition

Those are possible discussion tracks, but they are not the default model. The standard entry point is a negotiated non-exclusive commercial source license.

Licensing FAQ

Can I modify it?

Yes, internal modification is expected under the commercial source model, subject to the signed agreement.

Can I ship it in a commercial game or app?

Yes. The intent is to allow integration into customer products while restricting resale of UPE itself as a separate engine offering.

Do I own my own game or app?

Yes. You own your own product and your own content; the restriction is on the engine IP itself, not on your shipped application.

Is support included?

Only if that is part of the negotiated scope. Some deals may include onboarding or limited support; others may be delivery-only.
