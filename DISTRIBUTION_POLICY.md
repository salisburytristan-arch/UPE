# Distribution Policy

Unified Physics Engine (UPE) is distributed as proprietary source under private evaluation or commercial license terms.

## Allowed Distribution
- Private sharing with authorized evaluators in the same purchasing process.
- Internal use by licensed teams under signed commercial terms.

## Not Allowed
- Public reposting or mirroring of source package contents.
- Redistribution under open-source terms.
- Use outside the agreed license scope.

## Package Integrity
- Use `tools/hash_package.*` to verify package hashes.
- Keep `proof/` artifacts intact when sharing with evaluators.

## Support Model
- The package is designed for self-serve validation with build/test/proof scripts.
- No implicit support SLA is provided unless explicitly stated in commercial terms.
