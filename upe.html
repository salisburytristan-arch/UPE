Overview

What exactly buyers are looking at

UPE is a deterministic native physics and simulation runtime focused first on trust: repeatable stepping, replay and snapshot tooling, measurable proof scenes, and a clean integration surface. The core buyer story is not “everything imaginable,” but a serious engine with evidence and a straightforward licensing path.

Target audience

Game engine teams, rollback-heavy gameplay systems, simulation products, robotics/control experiments, and internal toolchains that need an auditable native runtime.

Maturity statement

The deterministic core and proof-oriented workflow are the flagship sale story. Applied packs and Labs are surfaced with honest maturity segmentation.

Commercial posture

Source licensing by default, ownership retained by BearPack/Arctic Codex, customer products may ship compiled integration, and resale of UPE itself is restricted.
