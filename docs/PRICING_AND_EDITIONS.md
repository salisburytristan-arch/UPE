# Pricing and Editions

This document is a draft packaging reference, not a binding price list. Final rights, scope, warranty, support, and transfer terms belong in signed commercial documents.

## Draft Packaging Tiers

### Evaluation

- Source review and internal technical diligence.
- Proof artifacts, benchmark logs, and docs.
- No redistribution rights.

### Studio

- Source integration for a single studio or project family.
- Commercial use in shipped products.
- Support terms negotiated separately.

### Enterprise

- Broader deployment scope or multi-team use.
- Higher support and diligence expectations.
- Usually paired with custom legal terms.

### Acquisition / Exclusivity

- Separate transaction structure if a buyer wants ownership transfer or exclusivity.
- Requires custom legal and commercial review.

## What Is Included In The Commercial Surface

- Deterministic runtime source.
- Buyer-verifiable proof docs and benchmarks.
- Example programs and integration notes.
- Maintenance and support terms as negotiated.

## What Is Not A Promise

- Public price tags.
- Unlimited support.
- Unrestricted redistribution.
- Automatic exclusivity.

## Recommendation

Use this doc as the high-level packaging reference and keep the operative deal terms in the license agreement, order form, or separate asset purchase documents.
